  • Inside the arc is 1, behind it is 2. Score and you keep the ball.
  • Only the defense can grab a miss. After a steal or rebound, take it behind the arc before you shoot.
  • Steals are reach, not luck. Get close on the side he is dribbling and reach when the ball is marked. Reach from too far, or across his body, and you whiff and get beat.
  • Protect the ball. Dribbling or sprinting into a defender puts it in his hands. Cross over to the far side when he sits on it.
  • Ankles: get him moving one way, then go back the other. Catch him leaning and he goes down.
  • Blocks are timing. Be near the top of your jump when he lets it go.
  • A clean green always goes in. A hand in your face shrinks the green.
  • Hit 3 in a row and you are on fire. Fill the hype bar for 10 seconds of takeover.
  • Close to the rim with a good release and nobody in front: dunk.
